# Validation protocol for leave-one-out dual prices

Fixed before inspecting the new samples or wind audit outcomes on 2026-10-31. The rule, cost penalties and validation target follow the preceding exclusion study. Each face is the coalition of all producers except one. Fit a scalar quantile and its bounded dual price on the pilot observations of each face. Freeze every price before validation. The target includes both the true largest saving B and the population gap D1-B of the level-one relaxation.

The six are pilot-fit face prices, one pilot-fit grand price with the same paired moment technique, forecast-fit face prices with the full 2M validation budget, the original fixed-forecast moment bound on 2M observations, and exhaustive DKW enumeration on M and on 2M observations. The last two provide a same-validation and an equal-total-budget comparison. Use identical observations wherever a method's statistical assumptions permit. The implemented offer and shares stay fixed for all six. Optimize neither the rule nor a method's hyperparameters on validation.

Report population structural error separately from validation uncertainty. Store exact population B and D1, face intervals, observed coverage failures, and mean excess upper bounds above B. Normalize by A, the largest possible loss on the frozen grand offer. Report both the certified structural gap and interval width against 5% of A. This threshold is a screening convention. The per-instance confidence budget covers six methods and three prespecified sample sizes. Use the minimum of Hoeffding and empirical Bernstein radii by splitting the one-sided event budget between them.

Proceed with this particular face-price method as the main scalable precision candidate only if, at the largest budget on the 48 heterogeneous cases, (a) it certifies D1-B <= 0.05 A on at least half, and (b) its mean normalized upper slack is below the original full-budget moment reference. Report exhaustive full-budget precision irrespective of this gate. The design is exploratory across known distribution families, with fresh samples. Each population check compares the theoretical inequalities against stored or independently recomputed oracle quantities.
